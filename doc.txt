## 2. Shipping Policy

1. Inside Dhaka, standard delivery usually takes 1-3 business days.
2. Outside Dhaka, standard delivery usually takes 3-5 business days.
3. Express delivery may be available for selected locations.
4. Delivery charges depend on location and product weight.
5. Customers receive an order confirmation after successful checkout.
6. Customers can request order tracking using their order ID.
7. Delivery time may increase during public holidays or unexpected disruptions.

## 3. Return Policy

1. Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving the product.
2. Products must be unused and in their original packaging.
3. The product must include all original accessories.
4. Products damaged by customer misuse are not eligible for return.
5. Customers must provide an order ID and return reason.
6. Return requests are subject to product inspection and approval.
7. Certain personal-use products may not be eligible for return.

## 4. Refund Policy

1. Refund requests are reviewed after the returned product passes inspection.
2. Approved refunds are processed within 5-10 business days.
3. Refunds are sent through the original payment method whenever possible.
4. Cash-on-delivery refunds may require bank or mobile financial service details.
5. Delivery charges may be non-refundable depending on the reason for return.
6. Customers receive a notification after refund approval.

## 5. Warranty Policy

1. Warranty duration depends on the individual product.
2. Some products include a 6-month warranty.
3. Some products include a 12-month warranty.
4. Warranty coverage applies to manufacturing defects.
5. Physical damage, liquid damage, and unauthorized repairs are not covered.
6. Customers must provide proof of purchase.
7. Warranty claims require inspection by the technical support team.

## 6. Order Cancellation Policy

1. Customers can request cancellation before the order is shipped.
2. Shipped orders cannot normally be cancelled through the standard cancellation process.
3. Customers should contact support with their order ID.
4. Cancellation requests are subject to order status verification.
5. Approved prepaid order cancellations are eligible for refund according to the refund policy.

## 7. Payment Methods

TechNova supports:
- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- bKash
- Nagad
- Bank Card

Payment confirmation may take some time depending on the payment provider.

Customers should never share their PIN, OTP, password, or full card security details with support staff.
